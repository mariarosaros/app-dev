### Original Series (Films 1-6): The core theme is the failure of modern people to escape the inbred cannibal family (primarily Three Finger), who use elaborate traps and intimate knowledge of the West Virginia wilderness to kill them for sport and food. The films consistently end with the cannibals surviving and the threat being perpetually renewed, often signaled by a final survivor or two being stalked or a new victim arriving.

## 2021 Reboot Last episode (Film 7): This film concludes differently, shifting the antagonists to a cult called The Foundation. The protagonist Jen, ultimately achieves a definitive victory and escape by violently turning the tables on the cult's leader.
